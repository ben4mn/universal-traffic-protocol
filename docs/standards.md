# Standards and evidence map

Reviewed 1 October 2026. These are primary sources informing the proposal. Candidate mappings below are architectural hypotheses; no field-complete gateway or conformance claim is implemented in v0.1. An adapter must name its source edition and test every transformation, including unavailable values and timing semantics.

## Candidate translations and information loss

| Source concept              | UTP destination                           | Translation obligations / omissions                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SAE BSM or ETSI CAM         | `presence`                                | Convert units and time base; preserve unavailable sentinels; retain measurement quality. UTP omits many native fields such as brake state, path history, and special vehicle containers.                                                              |
| SAE SPaT + MAP              | `infrastructure_state` with `signal`      | Resolve map revision and movement/signal-group IDs. Convert timing estimates conservatively. UTP's four phases omit native distinctions such as protected/permissive movement; a gateway must mark unknown if the application needs that distinction. |
| ETSI DENM                   | `observation`                             | Retain event time and relevance. UTP's observation does not include DENM cancellation, negation, action IDs, forwarding, or full cause-code semantics. Not a lossless mapping.                                                                        |
| ETSI CPM                    | `observation`                             | Transform sensor-local coordinates to WGS84; preserve measurement time and propagated error. UTP omits sensor and perception-region containers. One record is not independent evidence of another sender's report.                                    |
| City parking feed           | `infrastructure_state` with `parking`     | Publish observed capacity and occupancy with an actual measurement time; account for stale feeds. No parking reservation is implied.                                                                                                                  |
| Physical sign inventory     | `infrastructure_state` with `stop_sign`   | Require exact resource identity, map revision, authorized inventory, and physical/legal consistency. A broadcast cannot repeal a sign.                                                                                                                |
| Experimental merge proposal | `coordination_offer` + `coordination_ack` | A laboratory advisory exchange; no claimed mapping to a certified maneuver coordination protocol.                                                                                                                                                     |

A gateway MUST keep an explicit loss report. It MUST refuse translations that would remove necessary permission distinctions, erase unknown values, reduce uncertainty, restart validity, or falsely inherit source authority. The external binding and provenance attestation needed to authenticate a translated message are future work.

## Traffic evidence and honest inference

[Duranton and Turner, _The Fundamental Law of Road Congestion: Evidence from US Cities_ (2011)](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.101.6.2616) studies road capacity and travel demand in US cities. Its result is relevant to the long-term evaluation of congestion interventions. It does **not** estimate the effect of UTP, autonomous fleets, cooperative signals, or this simulation. The concern that an efficiency gain could attract extra trips is an inference to test.

`p²` in [the protocol draft](protocol.md) is an elementary probability result for independently equipped pairs, not an empirical adoption-to-delay relationship. Bottleneck service-rate arguments are capacity accounting, not a forecast. No percentages of real-world delay reduction are claimed by the protocol.

This repository's demonstration should report its own measured toy-model outcomes with its assumptions visible. A scientific claim would need calibrated demand and geometry, validated control and behavior models, repeated seeds, mixed-fleet scenarios, realistic network impairment, person-level fairness measures, and external validation.

## Intellectual property and implementation

UTP's authored contract and reference code can be distributed under the repository's open-source license. That does not grant rights to redistribute third-party standards texts, imply the absence of patents, or make a native standards implementation certified. References link to publishers; the repository does not copy their message definitions or ASN.1 modules.

The project does not claim a globally registered application identifier, a licensed radio allocation, a certificate authority, or authorization to control public infrastructure. Those are explicit deployment prerequisites outside this research release.
